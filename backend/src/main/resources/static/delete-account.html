Delete Your CaterHub Account

If you want to delete your CaterHub account and associated personal data, you can submit a deletion request below.

What happens when you delete your account?

Your CaterHub customer account is deactivated and personal identifiers (name, phone, email, profile details) are anonymized. Active login and refresh tokens are revoked.

Booking and transaction records may be retained for legal compliance, accounting, fraud prevention, and dispute resolution obligations. Retained records keep only the minimum necessary data.

Worker/partner and admin accounts are not deleted using this customer page and require a separate support-assisted process.
